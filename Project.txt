2. What are we actually building?
There will be four major user roles.
👤 Patient
Patient can:
- Register/login
- View profile
- View medical history
- View medications
- View previous treatments
- View appointments
- Book appointments
- Cancel/reschedule where permitted
- View doctor availability
- Use AI-assisted intake
- Upload relevant information/images where supported
- Receive notifications
👨‍⚕️ Doctor
Doctor can:
- Login
- View dashboard
- View assigned/current patients
- View patient history
- View appointments
- Manage availability
- Manage treatment information
- Prescribe/record medications
- Add medical notes
- Manage follow-ups
- View calendar
👩‍⚕️ Nurse
Nurse can:
- View assigned patients
- View treatment instructions
- View medication schedules
- View assigned tasks
- Update task/checkup status
- View patient information according to authorization
👨‍💼 Admin
Admin can manage:
- Users
- Doctors
- Nurses
- Departments
- Wards
- Doctor availability
- Assignments
- Hospital-level configuration

3. The AI Feature
This is one of the major differentiators.
Patient opens:
"Need Medical Assistance?"

They get an intake form.
Symptoms
Duration
Severity
Existing conditions
Current medications
Previous relevant history
Optional image/document

Then:
                    Patient Input
                         ↓
                Spring AI Service
                         ↓
                    External LLM
                         ↓
               Structured Response
                         ↓
             HealthSync Business Rules
                         ↓
        ┌────────────────┼─────────────────┐
        ↓                ↓                 ↓
    Emergency         Urgent            Routine
        ↓                ↓                 ↓
 Emergency          Department        General
 workflow           + Doctor          guidance/
                    availability       appointment

For example, an emergency workflow could trigger an emergency alert, notify appropriate available hospital staff, and direct the patient to emergency care rather than putting them through normal appointment booking.
Again, this would be implemented as an AI-assisted workflow, not an autonomous medical diagnosis system.
